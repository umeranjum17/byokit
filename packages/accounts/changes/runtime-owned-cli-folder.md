Remote CLI starts support cancellation during URL parsing and orderly `close()`, with a read-only lifetime `signal` aborted as soon as shutdown begins. Ready native settlement wins over phone cancel; shutdown still wins over late status and waits for settlement. Shell and remote starts share `.byokit-signin-lock`: the shell records its native child PID, while the remote driver records its HOST PID before spawn and retains it unchanged until row settlement and unlock. Dead holders are reclaimable; live or unproven holders stay busy. Exit-0 logins settle even without a phone waiter; failed initial holder writes release admission without spawning. Long waits share one child-exit observer. Detached settlement/cleanup errors return plain failures without crashing the host; fresh failed claims remove their new rows, and only fully settled sessions are evicted.
