- FIX: A refresh that gets no answer (a network failure) or a provider server error (5xx) no longer forces a new sign-in on phones and in browsers: the sign-in is kept and its stored grant is tried again on the next refresh. A provider refusal (`invalid_grant`, 400/401/403), any other answer that may have spent the grant (including an accepted answer that cannot be read), or a failed save still asks to sign in again.
