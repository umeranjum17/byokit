- FEAT: OpenClaw kit S8 `kit.removeAccount(member, id)`: a member-agent account goes through `models.authLogout` with its sealed profileIds, then its entry is dropped; an account agent goes through `byokit.accounts clear`, an empty `list` (a profile still listed after clear stops with nothing deleted and says the account is still signing in), `agents.delete` with `deleteFiles: false`, removal of its `<root>/state/agents/<agent>` dir, then the entry. Removal is refused `busy` while a run holds the agent and the member stays ready. On every `prepare` and after the sealed auth store restores, the kit removes each `<member>--<hex6>` state dir that a removal recorded as gone and no index names; a legacy member's own dir is never recorded and stays. `byokit.accounts clear` takes an optional `provider` that clears only that provider; a seal failure on a member-agent sign-in clears only that provider, and when that clear is refused the add reports failed with its credential still on the member agent.
- SECURITY: `kit.removeAccount` re-seals the live credential store at once while the engine runs, so `auth-store.sealed` and `auth-store.objects/` stop holding the removed profile immediately instead of keeping it until the next stop; a late engine write of the removed agent is swept on the next `prepare` or sealed restore.
