- FIX: A first start that misses its gateway handshake recovers by itself. A handshake timeout, or a transport that closes before hello, now moves the state to `restarting` with `retryAt` on the same backoff as a reconnect after `ready` (1 s, 2 s, 4 s, capped at 30 s), then starts the engine again; the fifth consecutive failed start moves to `failed` with why `handshake`, as before. `start()` settles once the kit is ready or has given up, `stop()` cancels a pending retry, and the new `KitOptions.handshakeMs` (default 90 000) sets the hello deadline per attempt.
