- FIX: A first start that misses its gateway handshake recovers by itself. A handshake timeout, or an engine exit before hello, now moves the state to `restarting` with `retryAt` on the same backoff as a reconnect after `ready` (1 s, 2 s, 4 s, capped at 30 s), then starts the engine again; the fifth missed attempt moves to `failed` with why `handshake`, as before. Each `start()` call has its own five attempts and settles once the kit is ready or has given up, `stop()` cancels its pending retry, and the new `KitOptions.handshakeMs` (default 90 000) sets the hello deadline per attempt.
