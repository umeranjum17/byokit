Bring your own AI plan and devices.

An open-source toolkit for apps that run on the user's own subscriptions.

byokit is a set of TypeScript packages for apps that run on the person's own AI plan and their own devices. An app signs in with the plan the person already pays for, pairs their phone with their computer over one encrypted link, and keeps every credential on the person's own devices, in the app's own store.

byokit operates no service. It ships the plumbing and nothing else: no account server, no credentials in the cloud, and no data collected about the person using the app.

What's in the toolkit

  • Sign in with the AI plan and Google account the person already has, on their own devices.
  • Pair a phone with a computer over one end-to-end encrypted link.
  • Package the whole kit for Node, browsers, iOS and Android from one codebase.
